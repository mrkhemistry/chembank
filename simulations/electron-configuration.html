First In, First Out

4s fills before 3d, yet on ionisation 4s empties before 3d. Same electrons, opposite order, because the levels swap once 3d is occupied.

They overlap

Normally shell n = 3 is below n = 4, but 4s and 3d are very close and overlap, which is why 4s can dip below 3d while empty.

Why 4s rises

Once 3d is occupied, those 3d electrons sit closer to the nucleus and repel the 4s electrons outward, raising 4s above 3d. So 4s comes off first.

Cr and Cu exceptions

A 3d⁵ or 3d¹⁰ set is extra stable, so Cr is 3d⁵4s¹ and Cu is 3d¹⁰4s¹, not the "expected" 3d⁴4s² / 3d⁹4s².
